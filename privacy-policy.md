# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 May 2026**

Dooit is an app developed and maintained by **Mikołaj Bryksa**. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. For any privacy-related question or request, please use the Contact form in the app.

### 1. Data Collected

- **Account** — when you first open the app, an anonymous account is created so your data can be saved and synchronised.
- **User name** — the name you enter during onboarding or change later in Settings. It is stored on your device and in the app's database, linked to your account. You may use any name, including a nickname.
- **Habit data** — habits you create, completion history, and streak statistics, linked to your account.
- **Error reports** — if the app crashes or hits an error, it records the error message, app version, and the screen where it occurred.
- **Contact submissions** — if you use the in-app Contact form, the email address and message you provide are stored so the author can respond. This links your email to your account for as long as the submission is retained.
- **Advertising data** — ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, IP address, and device information. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app does **not** collect phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or biometric data.

The app's database is hosted by Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy). Some data may be processed outside your country by this provider, under standard data-protection safeguards. Data is encrypted in transit, and each account can only access its own records.

### 2. How Data Is Used

All data is used to help you build habits — that is the main purpose of the app. Specifically, data is used to:

- Save your habits and progress across sessions.
- Personalise the interface with your chosen user name.
- Diagnose and fix bugs.
- Respond to Contact form submissions.
- Display advertisements (via Google AdMob).

The author does not sell your data or share it with data brokers. Some advertising data collected by Google AdMob may be shared with advertising partners for ad delivery — this can be managed through the consent dialog shown by AdMob on first launch and in the Settings view.

### 3. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the data held about you.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your data.
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
- Opt out of the sharing of personal information for advertising — manageable through the consent dialog shown by Google AdMob.

To exercise any of these rights, use the Contact form in the app. Requests are answered within 30 days.

### 4. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all your data at any time from Settings. This removes your habits, streak, user name, error reports, and contact submissions from both the database and your device. The action is **irreversible**.

All data is also deleted automatically after 12 months of inactivity.

Deleting your Dooit data does not affect data previously collected by Google AdMob. To manage Google's data, visit https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy
